1. Product Vision
Product Name

ERP Pulse

Tagline

AI-Powered NetSuite Intelligence Platform

Vision

ERP Pulse is a multi-tenant SaaS platform that extends NetSuite with AI, OCR, Analytics, Reporting and Business Intelligence.

ERP Pulse does not replace NetSuite.

NetSuite remains the transactional ERP.

ERP Pulse becomes the Intelligence Layer.

2. Problem Statement

Companies using NetSuite still perform many repetitive and manual tasks.

Examples

Manual invoice entry
Difficult report generation
Complex saved searches
Lack of AI insights
Limited dashboard customization
No OCR workflow
No executive analytics
Manual business monitoring

ERP Pulse solves these problems.
